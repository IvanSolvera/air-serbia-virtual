Palette & construction

The mark is built from three mirrored chevrons on a 120-unit grid: the upper chevron splits into two heads (the double-headed-eagle nod), the broad middle chevron reads as spread wings, and the lower crimson chevron is the tail / Serbian-red accent. Wordmark set in Sora ExtraBold; “VIRTUAL” is letter-spaced in crimson. Fully symmetric, scales cleanly to 16px.
